# Responsible use

DeepFake Sentinel is a screening and decision-support system. Its output is a model signal, not proof of authenticity or manipulation. Deepfake detectors can fail on unfamiliar generation methods, and compression, cropping, blur, lighting, and limited facial visibility can materially change their output.

Attention overlays show regions that influenced the configured Vision Transformer. They are not pixel-level forgery masks. Never use a score or overlay as the sole basis for legal, employment, financial, safety, or other consequential decisions. Preserve source media and chain-of-custody information outside this prototype when expert verification is required.

Raw uploaded media is never sent to OpenRouter. The optional explanation service receives only structured detector output and textual review context.
